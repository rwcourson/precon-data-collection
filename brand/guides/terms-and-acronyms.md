## Common acronyms (meaning & stylization)
This list clarifies meaning and styling — not a mandate to use them. Use discretion per pursuit.

**Special B&G stylizations (get these right):**
- **BGES** → write as **"B&G Equipment and Supply."** (Internal: spell out first, then "BGES" or "the shop"; never "the shop" externally.)
- **FSED** (freestanding emergency department) → stylize as **"freestanding ED."**
- **VDC** → generic "virtual design and construction"; our department "Virtual Design + Construction."
- **LEED®** and **LPS®** → always carry the ® on first use per page.
