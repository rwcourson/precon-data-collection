# Brasfield & Gorrie Slideshow Quick Start

This package contains the current 2026 visual and verbal brand resources supplied for presentation-making.

## Start here

- Use the horizontal navy logo on white or light slides.
- Use the horizontal white logo on navy, dark, photographic, or video backgrounds.
- Use Sharp Grotesk PE SmBold 15 for slide titles and Sharp Grotesk PE Book 20 for body copy.
- Lead with Blue 4 (`#002070`) and white. Use Blue 3 (`#0028F0`) or Blue 2 (`#3888FF`) sparingly as accents.
- Keep corners square, use hairline rules, avoid gradients and decorative shadows, and leave generous white space.
- Prefer the master brand for external/client-facing presentations. Regional marks are generally for regional/internal material; state marks require regional-leadership approval.

## Recommended slide setup

- Widescreen: 16:9
- Title: Sharp Grotesk PE SmBold 15
- Body: Sharp Grotesk PE Book 20
- Light slide: white background, Blue 4 title, black body, restrained Blue 3 accent
- Dark slide: Blue 5 (`#00143C`) or Blue 4 background, white text, restrained Blue 2 accent

## Important usage notes

- Always write “Brasfield & Gorrie” with the ampersand. Do not use “B&G” as company shorthand.
- Do not recolor, distort, outline, rebuild, or place the logo in a contrast-forcing box.
- Respect clear space equal to the height of the “B” in “Brasfield.”
- Minimum logo sizes: horizontal 1.25 inches or 200 pixels; stacked 0.75 inches or 100 pixels.
- Green is not a primary brand color in the 2026 system. Secondary colors are for charts, site logistics, and pursuit utility graphics.
