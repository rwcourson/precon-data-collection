Brasfield & Gorrie brand assets
Logos: logos/<type>/bg-<type>-<color>.<svg|png> (navy is the default/primary).
Use the logo color that fits the background: navy on light, white on dark/photo, black for mono.
Regional marks: logos-regional/<region>/*.png — badges are for field use (signage/merch),
not corporate/presentation materials; state marks need regional leadership approval.
Magnus: logos-magnus/magnus-symbol-<color>.<svg|png> — the 'm' symbol only. Magnus Blue
(#1DFFFF) on dark/navy digital surfaces, Blue 3 (#0028F0) or Blue 5 on light, white on
photography, black for mono print. Magnus Blue does NOT reproduce in CMYK print. The
'magnus' wordmark and the icon+wordmark lockup are not included — request that artwork.
Fonts: Sharp Grotesk PE TTFs. brand.css and brand-tokens.json hold the colors/type tokens.
